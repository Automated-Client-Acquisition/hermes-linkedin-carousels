# Post-pattern registry

- breakdown: Deconstruct a process, framework, or result step by step.
- reframe: Take a commonly held belief and flip it to reveal what actually matters.
- story: A brief narrative with a clear operational lesson.
- observation: A sharp take on a trend or pattern you're seeing.
- system-reveal: Walk through a system or stack you built, component by component.
- free-frame: Position the post as giving away something you normally charge for.
- fascination-bullets: Brunson-style curiosity bullets that tease without resolving.
