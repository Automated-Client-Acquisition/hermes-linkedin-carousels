# Pattern: free-frame

Position the post as giving away something the user normally charges
for. Raw, generous, slightly irreverent. Breaks the expected sales
pattern and gets engagement signals plus DM access in return.

## When to use

- The user actually has a paid service or audit that maps to the free
  thing they're giving away.
- The free thing is real and ready to ship.
- The voice is `hype`. `senior-operator` rarely uses this pattern (the
  voice doesn't say "fuck it"). `clean` softens to "I usually charge
  for this; here it is."
- The hook is `stop`, `never`, `credibility`, or `bold-claim`.

## Structure

```
What you normally charge for (1 line).

The pivot (1 line, often profane in hype voice).

Context for why this matters right now (1-2 lines).

What's inside the free thing (numbered list or arrows, 4-7 items).

Time-bound framing (1 line, optional, e.g. "free for the next 48 hours").
```

## Worked example

```
I charge for [this service/audit/system].

But fuck it. Here's everything I know for free.

[Problem context. Why this matters right now.]

Inside:
→ [item 1]
→ [item 2]
→ [item 3]
→ [item 4]

Sharing this free for the next 48 hours.
```

## Rules

- "But fuck it" only in `hype` voice. `clean` uses "But this matters
  enough to share without the gate." `senior-operator` rarely uses the
  pattern at all.
- The free thing is real. Don't promise a doc you haven't written. Don't
  promise an audit you can't deliver.
- The inside-list is fascination-bullet style. Tease, don't resolve.
- Time-bound framing is optional but works ("free for the next 48
  hours", "first 10 people get it"). Only use if the time-bound part is
  real.

## What NOT to do

- Don't use this pattern if the paid version doesn't actually exist.
  "I'd normally charge for this" is fiction if there's no rate card.
- Don't stack profanity. One "fuck it" maximum.
- Don't add a sales pitch after the giveaway. The whole point is the
  giveaway is the only ask.
- Don't promise more than the free thing actually contains. The doc has
  to match the bullets.

## Pairs well with

Hooks: `stop`, `never`, `credibility`, `bold-claim`. CTAs:
`keyword-capture` (the gate is just a comment, which is the joke) or
`generosity-frame` (the explicit "f*ck it, free" CTA, which doubles
down).
