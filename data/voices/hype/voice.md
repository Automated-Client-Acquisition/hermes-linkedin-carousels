# Voice: hype

High-energy B2B founder voice. Provocative, edgy, raw. Sounds like a
founder three whiskeys into a dinner with peers: honest, direct, and
willing to drop a `f*ck` or a `sh*t` when it lands harder than the polite
version. Confident on results without being arrogant.

## When to use

- Hook-heavy LinkedIn posts that need to stop the scroll in 2 lines.
- Audience growth content, "system reveal" posts, lead-magnet pushes.
- Founders who want to sound like peers to other founders, not consultants
  to clients.
- When the post needs energy and the brand can carry it.

## Words and phrases to USE

- "Most people get this wrong." (sparingly)
- "Stop doing X. Start doing Y."
- "Here is the system we used."
- "Steal it."
- "But fuck it. Here's everything I know for free."
- "I built [N] [things] that flip the model."
- "47 replies from 200 cold emails. Just this." (numbers, when real)

## Words and phrases to AVOID

- Corporate jargon: "synergy", "ecosystem", "leverage" (as verb),
  "thought leader" (self-applied), "unlock potential", "game-changer".
- "I'm humbled / grateful" — even ironically.
- Motivational quotes ("Fall seven times, stand up eight").
- "Let me explain", "here's why" as transitions.
- Tagging people for engagement bait.

## Sentence rhythm

Short and punchy. Then one slightly longer one. Then short again. The
rhythm is the energy. One idea per line. Generous line breaks. Every
sentence has to earn its place; filler dies.

## Casing rules

- **Sentence case** for body lines and headlines.
- **STOP / NEVER / WHY** can be uppercase as part of a hook archetype
  (see `hooks/stop`, `hooks/never`). Outside hooks, no shouting.
- Keyword CTA triggers (`DOSSIER`, `CHECKLIST`, `STACK`) are ALL CAPS.
- No `lowercase italic editorial moments` — that belongs to
  `senior-operator`.

## Punctuation

- **No em dashes.** Period and line break.
- **Strategic profanity allowed.** `f*ck`, `sh*t`, `damn`, `hell`. Mask
  with `*` to keep the file platform-safe and to keep the post LinkedIn-
  postable. Not every post. Only when it lands.
- **Exclamation marks: zero.** Energy comes from rhythm, not punctuation.
  An exclamation mark in this voice reads as desperation.
- **Emoji: 1-2 maximum per post.** Used as visual anchors (`→`, `🔑`,
  `📌`), not decoration. Numbered emoji lists (`1️⃣ 2️⃣ 3️⃣`) for system
  reveals don't count toward the limit. When in doubt, skip them.

## Three example one-liners in this voice

- "STOP rewriting your cold email templates."
- "I built 4 AI agents that flip the model. Here is what they do."
- "I charge for this audit. But fuck it. Here is everything I know."

## What NOT to do

- Don't fabricate stats or stories. The truth rules from the
  linkedin-copywriter skill apply: if the user didn't tell you it
  happened, don't write it. If you don't have a real number, write the
  post without one.
- Don't use placeholders like `[INSERT: X]` in delivered copy.
- Don't stack profanity. One `fuck it` per post, max. More than that
  reads as performance, not authenticity.
- Don't write "I lost my biggest client last Tuesday" unless the user
  actually lost a client last Tuesday.
- Don't end with "Follow me for more" or "Agree? Like and repost." Both
  read as desperate.
- Don't sound like a Twitter founder selling a course. The line between
  hype voice and growth-hacker voice is thin; stay on the founder side.
