# Voice registry

The authoritative list of voices that `init.py --voice <name>` accepts.
Each line: `- <name>: one-sentence description`. Adding a voice means
adding a folder under `voices/<name>/` AND adding a line here.

- senior-operator: Dry, declarative, anti-hype. Senior B2B operator. Periods do the work, no exclamations, no emoji.
- hype: High-energy founder voice. Bold claims, short punchy lines, strategic profanity allowed when it lands.
- clean: Neutral, calm, authoritative. Measured consultative tone. Zero profanity, zero emoji.
