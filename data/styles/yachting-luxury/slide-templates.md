# yachting-luxury slide templates

## Hook slide
- Deep navy background (#0A1F3D)
- Large centered or left-aligned headline with one brass accent word
- Thin brass rule beneath headline
- Small kicker in muted sand at top
- Generous negative space

## Problem / Story slides
- Warm bone background (#F8F5F0)
- Ink navy body text
- Brass used only for one key phrase or thin rule
- Clean, editorial layout with breathing room

## CTA / Closing slide
- Deep navy background
- Large brass or white headline
- Thin brass rule
- Contact or next-step text in muted sand or white
- Page marker in brass

## General rules
- Always maintain generous margins (minimum 80px from edges)
- Never crowd the slide
- Brass accent should feel like polished hardware — small and intentional
- Dark and light slides should alternate naturally for rhythm
