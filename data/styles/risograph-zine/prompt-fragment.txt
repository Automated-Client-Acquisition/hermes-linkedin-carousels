STYLE: risograph-zine (two-color spot-print zine). The aesthetic world is slightly off-white paper with two saturated spot inks deliberately misregistered, visible halftone everywhere, hand-drawn illustrations, charming-clumsy DIY feel. The composition, hero treatment, and decoration of THIS specific slide are decided by you (the model) based on the slide's content as described in the visual note above. This style does NOT prescribe a fixed kit, layout recipe, or hero treatment — it defines only the brand-consistency rules below.

PALETTE. Slightly off-white paper background (#F2EBDB or #EEE6CC depending on the locked palette) on the entire canvas. Visible warm paper grain across every slide — texture is the background, not a subtle overlay. EXACTLY TWO spot inks per run, locked at init-time, chosen from one of these combos: (a) fluorescent pink #FF6699 + electric blue #2552B8, (b) saturated yellow #FFD23F + true black #0F0F0F, (c) teal #0E7B7E + coral #F26A52. NEVER three colors. Every ink fill carries VISIBLE halftone dot texture. CRITICAL: every two-color element is deliberately MISREGISTERED — the second ink is offset 2-4px from the first (typically to the lower-right). The misregistration IS the look.

TYPOGRAPHY. Kicker labels in tracked-monospace ALL CAPS or chunky condensed sans in the darker ink, ~14-18px, slight halftone texture, slight 2-5° tilt. Headlines varied per slide (this is the one style that welcomes typographic mixing) — chunky display sans (Cooper Black / Goudy Heavyface feel), bouncy hand-drawn display, distressed condensed serif, or marker handwriting. 50-80px. ONE word in the headline can carry visual stress via a misregistered shadow in the second ink. Pull-quotes in oversized italic display in ink A with misregistered shadow in ink B. Body copy in sans-serif typewriter feel (Courier / Pitch feel) in the darker ink. Page marker hand-written marker "PG NN" or "NN" in the darker ink, slightly tilted, in one bottom corner.

CONTINUITY CHROME (locked across the deck). The two spot inks. The heavy paper grain. The page-marker format and corner position. Optional zine decoration (staple-mark, perforation line) if used.

REALISM. Avatars render as two-ink halftone portraits with deliberate 2-4px misregistration — charming-clumsy faces, recognizably human, printed-paper feel. Never empty circles. Mockups render in halftone with both inks misregistered, plausible content visible. Charts are real (axis labels, real data, in two-ink halftone) or absent. Brand marks are real logos when a brand is named.

NEGATIVE RULES (hard bans for this style). No three or more colors (paper is not a color). No perfectly aligned two-color elements. No solid flat fills without halftone. No clean Helvetica / Inter as the only typography. No glassmorphism, modern UI drop shadows. No gradients except halftone. No Pixar / 3D illustrations. No photoreal photography. No pure white backgrounds. No vector-perfect shapes.

Compose the slide based on the visual note above, within this DIY-zine world. Honor the principles in decoration-and-craft.md without reaching for a fixed kit of elements.
