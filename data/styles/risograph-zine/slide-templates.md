# Slide-type templates — risograph-zine

## Hook slide (slide 1)

- TOP: kicker label in tracked mono, slightly tilted.
- HEADLINE: chunky display sans or bouncy display, 60-80px, with the
  misregistered-shadow trick on one word.
- HERO: ONE hand-drawn-feel illustration (a robot, a cassette, a hand,
  a lo-fi monitor) printed in both inks with visible misregistration
  and halftone fills.
- FOOTER: hand-written "PG 01" in marker, slightly tilted. Optional
  staple-mark in the opposite corner.

## Body slide (slides 2..N-1)

- TOP: kicker.
- HEADLINE: vary the type treatment per slide. Across the carousel,
  rotate between chunky display, bouncy display, distressed serif,
  typewriter monospace, and marker handwriting.
- HERO: pick the diagram type:
  - **Illustrated explainer** — one hand-drawn illustration printed in
    halftone with misregistration, with marker-handwriting labels.
  - **Polaroid moment** — a photo treated as halftone inside a
    polaroid frame, tilted, with a marker note beneath.
  - **Type-as-hero** — a single oversized word in italic display with
    misregistered shadow, no illustration needed.
  - **Comparison** — two illustrations side by side, each in one of
    the two inks, with a hand-drawn arrow between them.
- FOOTER: marker page marker.

## CTA slide (slide N)

- TOP: kicker "GET IT" or "WANT IT".
- HEADLINE: marker handwriting "comment <WORD>" with the trigger
  word knocked out of a halftone block.
- HERO: a hand-drawn-feel chat-bubble illustration printed in halftone,
  containing the trigger word in chunky display.
- FOOTER: marker page marker, optional zine sign-off ("RISO 2026",
  "PRINTED WITH LOVE").
