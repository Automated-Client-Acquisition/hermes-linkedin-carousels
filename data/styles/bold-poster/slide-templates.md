# Slide-type templates — bold-poster

## Hook slide (slide 1)

- Hero type: ONE oversized typographic statement. Pick the strongest
  treatment for the hook — usually italic display serif for an
  emotional pivot, or knockout type for a declarative claim.
- Alignment: left-aligned or bleeding off the right edge. Avoid
  centered on slide 1 (centered reads as default; the hook earns
  attention by breaking default).
- Word count: 3-6 words for the hook.
- Footer: page marker "01 / NN", optional wordmark.

## Body slide (slides 2..N-1)

- Hero type: vary the treatment per slide across the carousel —
  alternate italic serif, condensed sans, knockout type, and (rarely)
  a marker-handwriting moment for tonal contrast. Never use the
  exact same treatment twice in a row.
- Alignment: vary across the carousel — left / center / right / bleed.
- Word count: 1-6 words. Single-word slides are powerful when
  positioned as a beat ("Wait." / "Replace." / "Compound.").
- Footer: page marker.

## Mid-carousel reveal slide (optional)

- Pull-quote treatment: a single italic-serif sentence centered or
  left-aligned, with an oversized opening quotation mark in the
  accent color overhanging the first line.
- This is the emotional pivot of the carousel and should be placed
  around 60-70% through the deck.

## CTA slide (slide N)

- Hero type: a single declarative call rendered in knockout type or
  condensed display. "comment receive." or the trigger word itself
  rendered as the entire slide.
- The trigger word IS the slide. Render it at 140-200px, centered,
  in the accent color.
- Footer: page marker, no swipe affordance, wordmark.
