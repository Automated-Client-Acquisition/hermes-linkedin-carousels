STYLE: bold-poster (pure typographic poster). The aesthetic world is type-only — no diagrams, no illustrations, no content cards, no photographic elements. The TYPE is the slide. Negative space carries the weight. Swiss / Dutch poster tradition. The composition (which typographic treatment, what alignment, how the type bleeds or breathes) is decided by you (the model) based on the slide's content as described in the visual note above. This style does NOT prescribe a fixed kit, layout recipe, or hero treatment — it defines only the brand-consistency rules below.

PALETTE. Background locked per run to ONE of: warm off-white (#F4EFE5) for calm-confidence mood OR deep ink (#0C0C0C) for gravity mood. Default type is deep ink (#0C0C0C) on light, cream (#F4EFE5) on dark. ONE accent color locked per run — saturated red (#C8332B / #E63322), saturated yellow (#F2C744), or electric blue (#2752E6 on light / #4F7CFF on dark). The accent appears only as: one emphasis word per slide, optionally one heavy slightly-irregular underline beneath the keyword, and a tiny accent dot beside the page marker. NEVER as a background fill. Muted gray (#7A746A on light / #86807A on dark) for the page marker.

TYPOGRAPHY. Hero type is one of three treatments, picked per slide based on content: (a) oversized italic display serif (Tiempos Display Italic / Caslon Italic feel) at 90-140px lowercase or sentence case, (b) oversized condensed display sans (Druk Wide / Compressa feel, weight 800-900) at 100-160px ALL CAPS or sentence case, or (c) knockout type — a solid accent-color rectangle with ONE ALL-CAPS word knocked out through it. The hero type alignment varies per slide to avoid repetition (left, center, right, or bleeding off one edge). ONE emphasis word per slide via color shift OR underline (never both on the same word). Page marker "NN / NN" in tracked monospace muted gray with a tiny accent-color dot, positioned bottom-left or bottom-right (NEVER centered). Optional small brand wordmark in the opposite bottom corner.

CONTINUITY CHROME (locked across the deck). Background palette. Accent color. Page-marker format and corner position. Wordmark position if used.

REALISM. Avatars are almost always omitted in bold-poster (the style is type-only). The one exception: an oversized italic-serif initial inside a circular block as a typographic stand-in — NEVER an empty circle. Mockups and charts are not used in this style. If the slide needs a mockup or a chart, switch styles entirely.

NEGATIVE RULES (hard bans for this style). No diagrams of any kind. No illustrations. No photographic cutouts. No sketched icons. No kicker pills. No content cards. No ticker rows. No paperclips. No stamps. No post-its. No marker fills. No subtitles (the headline IS the entire message). No multiple accent colors. No centered everything (vary alignment across the deck). No Title Case. No drop shadows on type. No gradients. No glassmorphism. No 3D shapes.

Compose the slide based on the visual note above, within this typographic-poster discipline. Honor the principles in decoration-and-craft.md without reaching for a fixed kit of elements.
