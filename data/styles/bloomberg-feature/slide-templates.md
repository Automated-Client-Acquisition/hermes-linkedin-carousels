# Slide-type templates — bloomberg-feature

## Hook slide (slide 1)

- MASTHEAD: section number "No. 01 — <SECTION>".
- HEADLINE: massive condensed display, 2 lines, with one accent phrase.
- HERO: either (a) a desaturated photoreal cutout with ONE accent
  detail, or (b) a knockout-type block with the slide's key word.
  No pull-quotes on slide 1.
- FOOTER: "No. 01" with accent dot.

## Body slide (slides 2..N-1)

- MASTHEAD: section number.
- HEADLINE: condensed display, with accent phrase.
- HERO: pick the type:
  - **Stat slide** — oversized numeral 120-200px with a short
    italic-serif caption beneath.
  - **Object slide** — photoreal cutout in desaturated tones with
    one accent detail.
  - **Pull-quote slide** — oversized italic serif quote with
    oversized opening quotation mark in accent color.
  - **Comparison slide** — two desaturated cutouts side by side,
    each with a small mono caption beneath ("OLD / NEW", "BEFORE
    / AFTER").
- FOOTER: page counter.

## CTA slide (slide N)

- MASTHEAD: "No. NN — ACCESS".
- HEADLINE: condensed display, "Comment to receive." with
  "receive" in the accent color.
- HERO: a sketched chat-bubble icon containing the trigger word in
  ALL-CAPS condensed display, in the accent color. Optional small
  signature in the lower-right of the slide.
- FOOTER: "No. NN", no swipe affordance.
