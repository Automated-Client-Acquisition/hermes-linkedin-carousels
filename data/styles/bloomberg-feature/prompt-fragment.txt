STYLE: bloomberg-feature (magazine long-read). The aesthetic world is warm cream paper, oversized condensed display typography, and desaturated photoreal cutouts with one saturated accent breaking through. Premium magazine-feature register. The composition, hero treatment, and decoration of THIS specific slide are decided by you (the model) based on the slide's content as described in the visual note above. This style does NOT prescribe a fixed kit, layout recipe, or hero-zone treatment — it defines only the brand-consistency rules below.

PALETTE. Warm cream background (#F5F0E6) on the entire canvas. NEVER pure white. Subtle paper texture overlay, barely perceptible, suggesting premium uncoated stock. Deep ink (#0F0E0B) for default type. ONE saturated accent color locked per run — either yellow (#F2C744) for calm-authority mood OR red (#C8332B) for urgent-feature mood. Muted (#6F695A) for captions and meta. Sepia (#9C8466) for desaturated cutout tones. NEVER two accent colors in one run. Photographic elements render in desaturated sepia / B&W with ONLY ONE small detail in the locked accent.

TYPOGRAPHY. Section numbers in tracked-monospace "No. NN" at ~14px in deep ink. Headlines in heavy condensed display sans (Druk / Compressa feel, weight 800-900) OR condensed display serif (Caslon Condensed feel), 60-90px on 1-2 lines, sentence case or ALL CAPS, with ONE phrase in the locked accent. Pull-quotes in oversized italic serif (Tiempos / Caslon Italic feel) at 36-48px, often with an oversized opening quotation mark in the accent. Body copy in sans-serif (Söhne / Inter feel) at 18-22px with generous line height. Attributions in tracked monospace at ~12-14px mute color. Page counter "No. NN" in tracked mono ink with a tiny accent-color dot beside it.

CONTINUITY CHROME (locked across the deck). Same section-number format and position. Same page-counter format. Subtle paper texture consistent slide-to-slide.

REALISM. Avatars render as photoreal-feeling portraits in desaturated sepia / B&W with ONE small detail in the locked saturated accent (yellow tie, red collar, yellow pen). Never empty circles. Mockups carry plausible content in desaturated tones with real-looking data, real-looking headlines, real-looking dates. Charts are real (axis labels, real data, real trend lines) or absent. Brand marks are real logos when a brand is named.

NEGATIVE RULES (hard bans for this style). No pure white backgrounds (only cream). No two accent colors in one run. No saturated color photography. No italic serif as the entire headline (only for pull-quotes). No multiple stamps per slide. No hand-drawn marker fills. No black backgrounds. No Title Case across multi-word headlines. No curved swipe arrows. No drop shadows on type. No glassmorphism or 3D renders.

Compose the slide based on the visual note above, within this premium magazine-feature world. Honor the principles in decoration-and-craft.md without reaching for a fixed kit of elements.
