STYLE: aca (editorial brand from automatedclientacquisition.com). The aesthetic world is warm editorial paper, italic serif typography, restrained color discipline. The composition, decoration, and hero treatment of THIS specific slide are decided by you (the model) based on the slide's content as described in the visual note above. This style does NOT prescribe a fixed kit, layout recipe, or hero-zone treatment — it defines only the brand-consistency rules below.

PALETTE. Warm Bone paper background (#F2EEE6) on the entire canvas. Pure white (#FFFFFF) only inside content cards. Deep Ink black (#15140F) for default type. ACA Red (#D4361A) as the only chromatic accent, used as text color for emphasis phrases inside headlines, as the outline color of any red-outlined chrome elements, as the color of annotations and the page-counter arrow. Muted gray (#C9C2AE) for hairline rules. Captions / meta in muted (#6B6759) tracked monospace. A small saturated green (#2EA66F) status dot appears only inside the kicker pill. Never pure white page background. Never red background fills (except inside knockout-type blocks where red is the field and type knocks through). No gradients. No drop shadows on type.

TYPOGRAPHY. Headlines in ITALIC Instrument Serif (or close serif substitute) at display size, lowercase or sentence case, with deep Ink for most of the headline and ACA Red for one or two emphasis phrases inside the same headline (same italic, same size, same weight, switched to red mid-sentence). Body copy in plain sans-serif (Inter feel) in deep Ink. Kicker labels in tracked-monospace uppercase (JetBrains Mono feel, +0.1em letter spacing) inside small white rounded pills with a tiny green status dot. Page counter "NN/NN" in JetBrains Mono small-caps black followed by a straight ACA-Red right-arrow glyph (→) on body slides. Brand wordmark "aca." in italic display sans (Bricolage Grotesque feel) with the trailing period in ACA Red. The headline is the visual hero of every slide.

CONTINUITY CHROME (locked across the whole deck). Same kicker-pill format on every slide that uses one. Same page-counter format and position (corner-locked from slide 1). The "aca." wordmark appears once, on the closing slide.

REALISM. Avatars render as editorial illustration in Le Monde / The New Yorker portrait style — soft tonal washes, simplified human features. Never empty circles, monograms, or silhouettes. Mockups (email previews, profile cards, document mockups, dashboards) carry plausible italic-serif body content. Never lorem ipsum, never empty gray bars. Charts are real with axis labels and real-looking data, or absent — never fake ascending-arrow shapes pretending to be data. Brand marks are real logos when a brand is named on the slide.

NEGATIVE RULES (hard bans for this style). No pure-white page background (only Bone). No sans-serif headlines (italic Instrument Serif only). No em dashes in body copy. No exclamation marks. No Title Case. No curved or twisted swipe arrows. No hand-drawn marker fills (that's hand-drawn-saas). No halftone B&W photographic cutouts (that's noir-collage). No rubber stamps labelled FILED / MEMO / DOSSIER (the editorial-dossier vocabulary was removed from the brand). No emoji on slides. No "leads" as a noun for prospects (the brand uses "meetings", "buyers", "pipeline"). No "AI-powered" anywhere.

Compose the slide based on the visual note above, within this aesthetic world. The visual note describes what the slide is about; you decide how to render it.
