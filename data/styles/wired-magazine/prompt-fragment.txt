Design-forward magazine spread on clean white paper (#FAFAFA) — like a WIRED or Fast Company feature that a creative director marked up with a sharpie. Palette: near-black text (#0D0D0D), electric blue accent (#0055FF), muted labels (#707070), subtle hairline rules (#E0E0E0). Bold condensed sans-serif display headlines with tight negative tracking and one accent-colored emphasis word per headline. Clean geometric sans-serif body copy — short, never paragraphs. Flat solid-color accent blocks behind white knockout text — no gradients, no drop shadows. Photography-forward with bold graphic overlays: solid accent rectangles, thin accent rules, geometric shapes intersecting type. Asymmetric layouts with generous negative space. CRITICAL: every slide must have at least one hand-drawn accent-color marker annotation — a circle around a key word, an underline beneath a phrase, a margin arrow, a checkmark, a star, or a scribbled note. Hand-drawn marks have visible marker texture (1.5-2px stroke, slightly imperfect, like a real sharpie on paper). Hand-drawn page counter in accent color, slightly tilted, lower corner. The energy is stylish and human — a designed magazine that someone actually touched, not a sterile corporate deck. High-energy, opinionated, kinetic. Negative: no warm paper, no cream or off-white backgrounds, no gradients, no drop shadows, no serif headlines, no halftone or film grain or risograph misregistration, no empty sterile slides without hand-drawn marks, no watermarks, no logos, no gibberish text, no Title Case headlines, no centered-everything layouts.
