# Slide-type templates — wired-magazine

Per-slide-type composition rules for the `wired-magazine` style.

## Hook slide (slide 1)

The cover — maximum impact, minimum elements, ONE bold hand-drawn mark.

- HERO: massive bold condensed sans-serif headline, 80-110px, broken
  across 2-3 lines, with ONE accent-colored emphasis word. The
  emphasis word gets a HAND-DRAWN marker circle around it — slightly
  imperfect oval in the accent color. The headline takes 60-70% of
  canvas.
- BELOW HEADLINE: single-line clean sans-serif body in near-black,
  ~24px, one supporting sentence.
- GRAPHIC ELEMENT: one solid accent-color block intersecting or
  underlapping the headline.
- Page counter bottom-right — hand-drawn marker-style "01 / 10" in
  accent color, slightly tilted.
- Kicker label top-left, small, in muted or as white-on-accent pill.
- NO photography on the hook slide — pure typography + one hand-drawn
  marker circle. The circle IS the human touch.

## Body slide (slides 2..N-1)

Feature-spread energy. Vary the layout AND the annotation type across body slides.

- KICKER: small tracked label top-left, either muted text or white
  text inside a solid accent pill.
- HEADLINE: bold condensed sans-serif, 48-64px, sentence case, one
  accent emphasis word.
- BODY: one supporting line in clean sans-serif, 20-24px, near-black.
- HERO ZONE (varies per slide):
  - Full-bleed editorial photograph with a graphic accent overlay
    AND a hand-drawn annotation (marker arrow pointing to key detail,
    circled element, margin scribble).
  - OR a clean UI mockup with hand-drawn accent arrows calling out
    features.
  - OR a clean vector chart with accent data lines and hand-drawn
    marker circles around key data points.
  - OR a split layout: 60% photo + 40% text with accent rule between
    and a hand-drawn underline beneath the key phrase.
- HAND-DRAWN ANNOTATION (MANDATORY, one per body slide, VARY THE TYPE):
  - Marker circle around emphasis word in headline
  - Hand-drawn underline beneath key phrase
  - Margin arrow in accent color pointing to important detail
  - Hand-drawn checkmark or star next to a key point
  - Scribbled accent-color note in the margin ("THIS" with arrow)
  - Cross-out / strike-through in accent marker on an old way/bad idea
- GRAPHIC ELEMENT: at least one geometric accent element per slide.
- Page counter bottom-right, hand-drawn marker-style or tracked mono.
  Consistent format across the deck.

**Annotation variety rule**: Never use the same annotation type on
consecutive slides. If slide 2 uses a marker circle, slide 3 uses
an underline, slide 4 uses a margin arrow. The annotations should
feel spontaneous, not scripted.

## CTA slide (slide N)

The closing spread — resolve the tension, deliver the ask.

- KICKER: small tracked label ("THE OFFER" or similar) with a hand-
  drawn star or checkmark next to it.
- HEADLINE: bold condensed sans-serif with the trigger word in the
  accent color. The trigger word gets a hand-drawn marker circle.
- BELOW HEADLINE: one line naming the deliverable, 20px clean sans.
- CTA ELEMENT: a solid accent-color pill/button with white sans-serif
  text "Comment [TRIGGER] →". A hand-drawn accent arrow points to
  the button from the left — like "← click this."
- NO swipe arrow on the final slide.
- Brand wordmark bottom-left, small and muted.
