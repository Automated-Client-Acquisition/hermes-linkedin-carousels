STYLE: noir-collage (investigative documentary). The aesthetic world is a near-black canvas with halftone B&W photographic evidence, red stamps, red annotations, and ALL-CAPS distressed serif headlines. Journalism-as-theatre. The composition, hero treatment, and decoration of THIS specific slide are decided by you (the model) based on the slide's content as described in the visual note above. This style does NOT prescribe a fixed kit, layout recipe, or hero-zone treatment — it defines only the brand-consistency rules below.

PALETTE. Near-black background (#0A0A0A, slightly warm, NEVER pure black #000000) on the entire canvas. Subtle film-grain overlay at ~5-10% opacity across every slide. Saturated red (#E63322) as the only chromatic accent, with deep red (#9A2014) for weathered stamp interiors. Muted gray (#807A70) for kicker labels, ticker numbers, page counter. Soft-white (#EDE9DF) for headline + subtitle text. Halftone cutouts use paper-white highlights (#F2EDE4) and ink (#0A0A0A) shadows. NEVER red background fills. NEVER pure black.

TYPOGRAPHY. Headlines in DISTRESSED CONDENSED SERIF (wood-type masthead feel, slight paper-print roughness on edges, slight ink imperfection, NEVER clean digital edges), ALL CAPS, large (60-80px), soft-white with ONE phrase per headline in saturated red. Subtitles in smaller ALL-CAPS sans (~18-22px), letter-spaced, soft-white at ~70% opacity, source-attribution register. Kicker labels in tracked-monospace uppercase muted gray. List items in bold condensed sans ALL CAPS soft-white with red underline beneath one keyword. Stamp text in slab-serif or condensed sans ALL CAPS red, tilted 8-12°, weathered/distressed ink edges. Ticker rows in tracked monospace muted gray. Page counter "NN/NN" in monospace small-caps muted gray.

CONTINUITY CHROME (locked across the deck). Film-grain texture, page-counter format and position, ticker-row format consistent across slides.

REALISM. Avatars render as halftone B&W photographic portraits with visible dot pattern, often side-profile or three-quarter angle (NEVER frontal headshots, NEVER empty circles). Mockups (document cutouts, news clippings, screen mockups, data tables) carry plausible content with halftone B&W treatment — real-looking journalism, real-looking dates, real-looking data. Charts are real (axis labels, real data) or absent. Brand marks are real logos when a brand is named.

NEGATIVE RULES (hard bans for this style). No pure black (#000000) backgrounds. No clean digital edges on headlines (must feel printed on rough paper). No italic serif headlines (that's aca). No hand-drawn marker fills (that's hand-drawn-saas). No cream / warm / beige backgrounds. No more than one rubber stamp per slide. No more than 4 halftone cutouts per slide. No curved or twisted arrows. No Title Case headlines. No friendly emoji. No red background fills.

Compose the slide based on the visual note above, within this investigative-documentary world. Honor the principles in decoration-and-craft.md without reaching for a fixed kit of elements.
