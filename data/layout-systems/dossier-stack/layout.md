# Layout system: dossier-stack

## The vibe

Capability-list teardown. The deck feels like the chapters of a
single dossier — body slides formatted in a similar rhythm so the
reader can scan a 10-capability stack at one pace. Continuity is
the look. The hook slide and the CTA break the rhythm; everything
between holds the line.

## When to use

- Capability-reveal posts ("here are the 10 things X does").
- System-reveal posts where the WHOLE point is the structured list.
- Comparison teardowns ("we tested 5 tools, here is what each
  does").
- Posts where the reader's job is to SCAN and EVALUATE, not to
  feel.

DO NOT use for: confessional narratives (`editorial-spread`),
breaking news (`newspaper-column`), terse field updates
(`dispatch-bulletin`).

## How the model uses this

dossier-stack is a vibe of structured continuity. The visual note
describes each capability slide's content; the model composes
visuals that read as part of a coherent stack within the locked
style's aesthetic world. The composition shape is consistent
across body slides; the decoration on each slide is content-driven
and varies based on what each capability is about.

There is no prescription of "every body slide uses a centered
white card with N number boxes." Those were defaults that produced
mechanical-looking decks. The model decides how to express the
capability within the locked style.
