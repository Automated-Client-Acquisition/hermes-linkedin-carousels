# Layout system: dispatch-bulletin

## The vibe

Field-report / wire-service grammar. The deck reads like a series
of typed field reports filed from somewhere serious. Terse,
operational, authoritative through restraint rather than through
decoration. Generous negative space. Tracked-monospace authority
chrome. Single hero element per slide. Short decks (6-8 slides)
work best — longer decks lose the wire-service feel.

## When to use

- Terse operational announcements ("we shipped X", "we are doing
  Y").
- "From the field" updates and founder letters.
- Posts that should feel like an internal memo accidentally made
  public.
- Posts where the brand voice is `senior-operator` and the topic
  is operational, not narrative.

DO NOT use for: long capability lists (`dossier-stack`),
confessional posts (`editorial-spread`), breaking news
(`newspaper-column`), single-quote manifestos (`editorial-spread`).

## How the model uses this

dispatch-bulletin is a vibe of operational restraint. The visual
note describes the slide's content (the dispatch head, the subject,
the body); you (the model) compose the slide that fits a wire-
service field-report within the locked style's aesthetic world.

There is no prescription of "every slide has a dispatch head + a
subject line + a left-aligned body block + a signature footer."
Those were defaults that produced visually identical slides. The
slide's content drives the actual composition. The shared vibe is
typed-on-a-telex restraint.
