# Layout system: editorial-spread

## The vibe

Magazine-feature spread. Each slide is a deliberate hero composition
that the editor laid out for THIS specific moment in the story.
Asymmetric weight, negative space carrying weight, no two slides
shaped the same. Some slides have a content card, some don't.
Some are pull-quotes. Some are oversized typographic moments. The
DECK reads as a sequence of editorial decisions, not a repeated
template.

## When to use

- Confessional / narrative-arc posts ("I was wrong about X", "here
  is what I learned", "the story behind the system").
- Posts with strong emotional pivots that need typographic moments.
- Posts where the reader should FEEL the deck rhythm shifting as
  they swipe.
- Founder essays adapted to a deck.

DO NOT use for: pure capability-list posts (use `dossier-stack`),
terse operational announcements (use `dispatch-bulletin`),
breaking-news / policy posts (use `newspaper-column`).

## How the model uses this

editorial-spread is a vibe, not a recipe. There is NO prescription
that "slide 1 uses hero-zone type A, slide 4 uses type D." The
orchestrator writes visual notes that describe each slide's
content, and the model composes the hero zone for that slide
contextually — within the editorial-spread mood of asymmetric,
non-templated, magazine-feature composition.

The locked style (aca, noir-collage, bloomberg-feature, etc.)
supplies palette + typography + voice. The editorial-spread vibe
supplies the compositional energy. The slide's content drives
everything else.
