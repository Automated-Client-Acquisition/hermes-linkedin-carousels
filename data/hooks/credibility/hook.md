# Hook: credibility

Lead with a verifiable result for a specific audience, then offer the
system on a silver platter. Built for lead-magnet posts where the
implicit ask is "let me hand you the playbook."

## Formula

```
Line 1: "We generated [specific result] for [specific audience]."
Line 2: "Here is the exact system we used. Steal it."
```

## When to use

- The result is real (truth rule applies).
- The audience the result was for is precise. Vague "businesses" doesn't
  hook anyone; "M&A advisors" or "$2-10M services firms" does.
- The post body actually walks through the system.
- Voice is `hype` (most natural) or `clean`. `senior-operator` can carry
  it but tones down the "steal it" bravado.

## Worked examples

```
We generated M&A advisors [N] additional engagements every year.
Here is the exact system we used. Steal it.
```

```
We booked [N] qualified meetings for $2-10M services firms in 90 days.
Here is the exact system we used. Steal it.
```

```
We generated [N] inbound replies from [specific industry] founders in one quarter.
Here is the exact system we used. Steal it.
```

## Common failure modes

- The audience is too broad. "We helped businesses grow" isn't a hook.
- The "system" in the post body is shallow ("we sent emails"). The
  credibility hook earns the right to teach, then has to actually teach.
- The numbers are inflated or rounded. Specific numbers beat round ones.
- "Steal it" gets softened into "consider trying this approach". The
  invitation is the energy.

## Pairs well with

`system-reveal` post pattern (the body IS the system) or `breakdown`
(the body walks the steps). CTAs: `keyword-capture` (the full system in
a doc) or `generosity-frame` (the "I charge for this, but here it is"
variant).
