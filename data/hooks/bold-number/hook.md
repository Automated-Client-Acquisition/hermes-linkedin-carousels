# Hook: bold-number

Lead with an impossibly specific result in a specific timeframe. Line 2
makes it feel impossible. Line 3 (where used) hands the reader the
"how" promise.

## Formula

```
Line 1: "[Specific result] in [specific timeframe]."
Line 2: "[One line that makes it feel impossible.]"
Line 3 (optional): "Here is how."
```

## When to use

- The result is real (truth rule applies).
- The combination of result + timeframe is genuinely impressive in the
  audience's domain. Calibrate.
- Voice is `hype` (best fit) or `credibility`-adjacent `clean`.

## Worked examples

```
[Currency amount] in [N] months.
From [unlikely channel]. Here is how.
```

```
[N] booked demos in [N] days.
From a list of [much smaller N] accounts. Here is how.
```

```
[N] qualified replies in [N] weeks.
Zero paid ads. Zero outsourced SDRs. Here is how.
```

## Common failure modes

- The number is fake or padded. The whole archetype dies if the
  audience sniffs inflation.
- The timeframe is too long. "$1M in 5 years" isn't a hook. "$85K in 6
  months from WhatsApp" is.
- Line 2 doesn't make the result feel impossible. The contrast between
  the result and the unlikely source is the whole hook; if the source
  is obvious, there's no tension.
- "Here is how" gets dropped and replaced with a sales pitch.

## Pairs well with

`breakdown` post pattern (the body IS the "how") or `system-reveal`
(the body walks through the system that produced the number). CTAs:
`keyword-capture` (the full system in a doc).
