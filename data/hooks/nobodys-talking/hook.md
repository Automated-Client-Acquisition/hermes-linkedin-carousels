# Hook: nobodys-talking

Create FOMO around a hidden opportunity. Line 1 declares the silence.
Line 2 names the opportunity in concrete terms. Line 3 (where used)
closes with "Until now."

## Formula

```
Line 1: "NO ONE is talking about this."
Line 2: "[Bold concrete claim about the opportunity.]"
Line 3 (optional): "Until now."
```

## When to use

- The opportunity is genuinely under-discussed in the audience's circle.
  If five other people posted about it this week, the hook is broken.
- The post body delivers the actual play.
- Voice is `hype`.

## Worked examples

```
NO ONE is talking about this.
You can message 20x more people on LinkedIn. Until now.
```

```
NO ONE is talking about this.
The new [specific API] lets you research accounts at scale for cents. Until now.
```

```
NO ONE is talking about this.
There is a way to inherit a competitor's inbound traffic. Until now.
```

## Common failure modes

- "Nobody is talking about this" is almost never literally true.
  Calibrate to your specific audience: if your circle hasn't noticed, the
  hook is honest. If LinkedIn at large hasn't noticed, that claim is
  bigger than you can defend.
- The opportunity in line 2 is hype, not substance. If the post body
  is "...and to learn more, DM me," the hook becomes ad copy.
- Reusing the hook too often. The whole point is rarity; if every other
  post is "NO ONE is talking about this", the brand becomes the boy who
  cried hidden opportunity.

## Pairs well with

`system-reveal` post pattern (the body teaches the play) or `breakdown`
(the body shows how to use the opportunity). CTAs: `keyword-capture`
(the system in a doc).
