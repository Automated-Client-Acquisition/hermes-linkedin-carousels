# Hook registry

- contrarian-punch: Challenge something everyone believes. Two lines that pit common wisdom against the truth.
- curiosity-gap: Open a loop the reader must close. The classic Brunson curiosity hook.
- curiosity-gap-misdirect: Misdirect away from the obvious cause and promise the real answer.
- bold-claim: Declarative statement that demands attention. No hedge.
- confession: Admit something unexpected. Vulnerability creates instant trust.
- specific-number: Lead with a precise number plus an unexpected outcome.
- pattern-interrupt-question: A question the reader has not considered.
- rip: Declare the old way dead. "R.I.P [old]. [New] just changed everything."
- stop: Command the reader to halt a common behavior. "STOP doing X."
- never: Promise the reader they will never suffer through a painful process again.
- bold-number: Lead with an impossibly specific result in a specific timeframe.
- nobodys-talking: Create FOMO around a hidden opportunity. "No one is talking about this."
- contrast: Juxtapose a painful before with an exciting after.
- credibility: Lead with a verifiable result for a specific audience, then offer the system on a silver platter.
